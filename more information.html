More Information

Below is the most common requested general information about Taniti.

  • Power outlets are 120 volts.
  • The drinking age on Taniti is 18.
  • No alcohol is served/sold between 12:00am and 9:00am.
  • English is spoken by some of the locals.
  • There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees. The hospital can be reached at +0 (123) 000-0000.
  • Taniti's main currency is the US dollar.
  • The island has several banks where currency can be exchanged.
  • Most businesses accept major credit cards.
  • For more information, please contact the Department of Tourism at +0 (123) 222-2222.
