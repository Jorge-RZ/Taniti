Beaches

Beaches

There are two main beaches on Taniti Island: Yellow Bay Beach and Rock Beach. Yellow Bay Beach has white sand and is in Taniti City. It has some vegetation, but it is very spacious for enjoying numerous beach activities. Rock Beach is farther south and closer to the rural areas of the island. As the name implies, it has small rocks and seashells that can often be found near the shore.

(Brown, 2009)
