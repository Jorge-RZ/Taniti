More Entertainment

Below is a list of more activities that can be found on the island.

  • chartered fishing tours
  • pubs/microbrewery
  • dancing at a new dance club
  • Watching a movie
  • helicopter rides
  • an arcade
  • art galleries
  • bowling

For more information about any of these activities please contact the Department of Tourism at +0 (123) 222-2222. A new nine-hole golf course is under construction but will be added to the list soon.
