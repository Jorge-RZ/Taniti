Landforms

Rainforest

A tropical rain forest encircles the volcano. The forest is filled with a wide variety of plants and animals. It has birds, lizards, and other small wildlife. Some areas are thick in vegetation so be sure to stick to the trails.

Volcano

As one of the main attractions of the island, this volcano has been here for millions of years. Truly a sight that can't be missed. A lot of the local folklore makes some mention of the volcano at the center of the island. A climb to the peak is available to the public. The trails are only open during daytime and close during holidays so make sure to contact the Department of Tourism for more information at +0 (123) 222-2222. A tourist guide can be appointed upon request to the department for a fee.
