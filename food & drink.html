Food & Drink

Resturants

The restaurants in Taniti City have the option of American style and pan-Asian meals, most of which can be found near the shopping area.

Local Dining

Be sure to try out the local specialty of fish and rice. Small ingredient list but bold flavor. There is also a local brewery and pubs.
