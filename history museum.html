History Museum

If you want a more tranquil vacation, why not visit the local museum? Whether you are interested in Taniti's past or present, the history museum is filled with artifacts and mementos important to the Taniti culture. The museum is open from 8 am to 5 pm, Monday through Friday. It will be closed on national holidays.

(Private Tours Hawaii, 2014)
